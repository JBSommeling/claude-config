description: Re-explain the previous answer in plain English — same facts, readable prose
---

Rewrite your previous explanation so it reads plainly. An argument after the command
names the part to rewrite; with no argument, rewrite the whole of the last explanation.

Work only from what is already in this conversation. Do not read files, run searches,
or re-derive anything — the content is settled, only the prose changes.

Rules for the rewrite:

1. **Keep every fact.** This is a rewrite, not a summary. Nothing is dropped because
   it is awkward to phrase, and nothing is softened into vagueness. Stated uncertainty
   stays uncertain.
2. **Lead with the conclusion.** The first sentence answers the question. Reasoning
   and caveats follow it.
3. **One idea per sentence.** Split anything that needs a second comma-clause or a
   dash aside to stay true.
4. **Gloss or drop the jargon.** A term the reader has not used themselves gets a
   plain-English gloss on first use, or it does not appear.
5. **Use concrete nouns and verbs.** "The hook blocks the push", not "the enforcement
   mechanism prevents the operation."
6. **Show one worked example** — a real command, real values, a real line of output —
   wherever a mechanism is being explained.
7. **Cut the throat-clearing.** No restating the question, no preamble, no closing
   summary.
8. **Simplify the shape, not the facts.** Order the ideas so each rests on the one
   before it. Name the part that matters most rather than giving every part equal
   weight. Detail that does not bear on the answer moves to the end — it is never
   deleted. The mechanism itself never changes: no rough approximation standing in
   for the real behaviour, no caveat dropped to make a sentence land cleanly.

An analogy is allowed when it sits beside the real mechanism, never in place of it.
Do not talk down and do not pad with reassurance.

If restating something plainly exposes a claim that was hand-waved or wrong, say so
rather than restating it faithfully.

Output only the rewritten explanation. Do not preface it and do not comment on the
original.
